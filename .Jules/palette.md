## 2026-11-09 - Accessible Form Inputs\n**Learning:** Relying purely on placeholder text for form fields creates an accessibility barrier for screen readers, and is also poor UX for sighted users who lose context once they start typing. The `contact` component had this issue.\n**Action:** Add explicit semantic `<label>` tags with matching `for` and `id` properties to all inputs, and use visual indicators (like `*`) to clearly denote required fields.
